Interpretation

    Early layers: PCA shows overlapping clusters, weak separation. These capture edges/textures, transferable but not highly discriminative.

    Middle layers: Clusters begin to separate, showing emergence of semantic abstraction.

    Final layers: Compact, well‑separated clusters. Strong semantic abstraction, high validation accuracy.

Representation hierarchy:

    Early features → general, transferable across tasks.

    Deeper features → task‑specific, higher accuracy but less robust to distribution shifts.

    Feature norms typically increase with depth, reflecting stronger activation magnitudes.

Robustness:

    Early layers provide resilience (generic features).

    Final layers are more sensitive but discriminative.

    Middle layers balance generality and specificity, often useful for transfer learning.
