Write-up / Analysis
1. Validation Accuracy under Corruption

    Accuracy drops as corruption severity increases:

        Gaussian noise: small σ (0.05) → mild drop; large σ (0.2) → severe degradation.

        Motion blur: reduces edge sharpness → hurts models relying on fine-grained texture cues.

        Brightness shift: moderate impact, but models with strong normalization layers (BatchNorm) handle it better.

2. Corruption Error (CE)

    Defined as 1−accuracy.

    CE grows with corruption severity.

    Motion blur often yields higher CE than brightness shift because blur destroys structural information.

3. Robustness Sensitivity (RS)

    Defined as accuracy under corruption/baseline accuracy.

    RS close to 1 → robust.

    RS much less than 1 → sensitive.

    Gaussian noise shows progressive RS decline with σ.

    Brightness shift RS is relatively stable.

🔹 Architectural Influence

    ResNet backbones: skip connections help preserve information flow, making them more robust to brightness shifts and moderate noise.

    Deeper models: more capacity, but also more sensitive to pixel-level noise (overfit to clean textures).

    Normalization layers: architectures with BatchNorm or LayerNorm adapt better to brightness changes.

    Convolutional hierarchies: early layers capture edges and textures; corruptions that destroy these (motion blur, high σ noise) cause larger drops.

🔹 Observed Patterns

    Noise sensitivity: accuracy decreases smoothly with σ, showing predictable degradation.

    Blur sensitivity: sharper drop, since blur removes discriminative features.

    Brightness robustness: relatively stable, especially in architectures with strong normalization.

    General trend: models that rely heavily on fine-grained textures (deep CNNs) are more vulnerable to blur/noise, while those leveraging global structure (skip connections, pooling hierarchies) show stronger robustness.

✅ In summary:

    Gaussian noise → gradual degradation.

    Motion blur → sharp accuracy drop.

    Brightness shift → mild impact.
    ResNet-style architectures demonstrate stronger robustness due to skip connections and normalization, but sensitivity patterns differ depending on corruption type.
